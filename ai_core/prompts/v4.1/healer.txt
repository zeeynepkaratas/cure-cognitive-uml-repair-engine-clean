Use UML notation consistently:
- A "1" --> "0..*" B expresses many B per A and one A per B. It does not

- A "1" *-- "1..*" B means A is the composition whole and B is its part.
- Only explicit composition/inheritance requirements justify those types.
  "Belongs", "uses", "has" and "manages" alone justify no stronger assumption.
- Preserve association direction and endpoint multiplicities supported by the SRS.
- Do not change a correct relationship merely because a Critic repeats an error.
